5 Million Promter / Dasturchi — TERMUX BUILD V3
================================================

Bu fayl borligi sizda YANGI versiya ekanligini isbotlaydi.

Tekshirish:
    cat VERSION.txt
Natija: "TERMUX BUILD V3" bo'lishi kerak.

V3 ichida:
    bot_offline.py          -> aiogram/pip kerak emas (asosiy bot)
    bot.py                  -> aiogram yo'q bo'lsa, o'zi bot_offline.py ga o'tadi
    termux_start.sh         -> faqat bot_offline.py ni ishga tushiradi
    termux_bg.sh            -> tmux bo'lmasa avtomatik nohup
    termux_nohup.sh         -> tmux'siz fon rejimi
    termux_stop_nohup.sh    -> nohup ni to'xtatish
    INSTALL_TERMUX.txt      -> yangi ko'rsatma

Eski belgilar (bularni ko'rsangiz — eski fayllardasiz):
    bot_offline.py topilmadi
    bot.py 4-qator: from aiogram import ...
    "Bot ishga tushyapti... (to'xtatish uchun Ctrl+C)"
    "Bot fon rejimida ishga tushirildi (tmux session: promter)."
